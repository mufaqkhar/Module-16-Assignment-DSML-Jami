# Research Proposal (Draft)

## Research question
Can standardized baseline BMI predict the quantitative diabetes disease progression score one year later using simple linear regression?

## Dataset
scikit-learn diabetes dataset, 442 patients. Source: https://scikit-learn.org/stable/datasets/toy_dataset.html#diabetes-dataset

## Planned approach
Use one predictor (BMI), fit an ordinary least squares model, and report held-out error and R².

## Existing work (draft)
1. Efron et al. (2004), *Least Angle Regression* — includes diabetes data as an example.
2. scikit-learn, *Linear Regression Example* — demonstrates a simple regression on diabetes data.
